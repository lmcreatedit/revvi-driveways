Answers & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Retaining Walls & Outdoor Steps process in Berkshire.

How do you prevent retaining walls from bowing or collapsing?

We anchor timber sleeper walls with vertical steel reinforcement posts concreted into the ground, install heavy-duty corner bracing, and backfill with clean aggregate and drainage pipe to prevent water pressure buildup.

Do I need building regulations for a garden retaining wall?

Retaining walls under 1 metre generally do not require building regulations. For higher tiering, we design step-terraced retaining systems that provide maximum soil retention safely within permitted limits.

Can you build raised vegetable and flower beds with sleepers?

Yes, sleeper planters are ideal for creating neat, raised flower beds and vegetable plots at comfortable working heights.
