Italian Porcelain (R11 Rated)

Answers & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Porcelain & Natural Stone Patios process in Berkshire.

What is the difference between porcelain and Indian sandstone?

Porcelain is vitrified ceramic that absorbs almost zero moisture (under 0.05%), making it completely stain-proof, scratch-resistant, and non-slip without requiring annual sealing. Sandstone offers traditional natural stone variation and texture but benefits from periodic sealing.

How long does a typical patio installation take?

A standard 30–50sqm patio typically takes 5 to 7 working days, including full excavation, MOT Type 1 sub-base compaction, drainage integration, mortar laying, and jointing.

Do you offer a guarantee on groundworks?

Yes, all our patios are laid on reinforced MOT Type 1 aggregate sub-bases compacted with heavy vibratory rollers, backed by our structural ground guarantee against sinking and pooling.
