WHO WE ARE · WOKINGHAM & BERKSHIRE

Built on British Standards. Backed by 15+ Years Experience.

Revvi Driveways was founded by Mike with a simple commitment: providing Berkshire homeowners with premium driveway and patio installations built properly from the ground up — with zero sales reps, zero subcontracting markups, and zero cutting corners.

Based in Wokingham (RG40) and covering Reading, Bracknell, and the wider Berkshire region, we specialize in high-load block paving, 100% UV-stable resin bound, machine-rolled tarmacadam, and vitrified porcelain patios. Every single project is engineered to British Standard BS 7533, including a full 200mm deep ground excavation and laser-compacted Type 1 MOT sub-base.

You deal directly with Mike from your free in-person site measurement to final handover. No pushy salesmen, transparent itemized quotes, and a multi-year structural workmanship guarantee.
