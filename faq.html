Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our UPVC spraying process, 10-year guarantees, kitchen resprays, and fast photo quotes.

All Questions

How much can I save by spraying instead of replacing?
Spraying typically saves between 70% and 80% compared to the cost of buying and fitting brand-new UPVC windows, composite doors, or full kitchen replacements. You keep your existing solid frames while getting a flawless, modern look.
How long does the sprayed finish last?
Our specialist architectural coatings chemically bond directly into the UPVC, composite, or wood surface rather than just sitting on top. They're engineered to last 15+ years and come backed by our comprehensive 10-year anti-fade, anti-crack, and anti-peel guarantee.
Will overspray get on my brickwork, glass, or garden?
Never. Over 60% of our on-site time is dedicated strictly to preparation. We use specialist electrostatic masking film and precision tape to completely seal off all glass, brickwork, sills, and surrounding areas before spraying starts. When we demask, your home is left spotless.
What colours and finishes can I choose from?
We offer over 1,500 colours, including standard RAL shades (like Anthracite Grey RAL 7016 and Jet Black) and exact matches to Farrow & Ball, Little Greene, and Dulux colour charts. Available in Matt, Satin (most popular), or Gloss.
How much disruption will there be in my home?
We keep disruption to an absolute minimum. We do as much prep and work outside as possible, only entering your living spaces when strictly necessary. Everything is sealed with protective sheeting, and we leave your home tidy and spotless at the end of every day.
Do you repair minor scratches, chips, or marks before spraying?
Yes. Multi-stage preparation is included as standard. We degrease, clean, and sand all surfaces, repairing minor dings and scratches so the final sprayed finish looks completely seamless and factory-fresh.
Can you spray both the exterior and interior of windows and doors?
Yes. Most homeowners spray the external faces to boost curb appeal, but we can also spray internal opening rebates, sills, and interior frames for a complete 360-degree colour change.
How do I get a quote?
Simply take a few photos of your windows, doors, or kitchen on your phone and send them to us on WhatsApp at 07477 510304. We will review your images and send back a fast, fixed written quote with zero sales pressure.
