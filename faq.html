All Questions

How much can I save by spraying instead of replacing?
Spraying typically saves homeowners between 70% and 80% compared to the cost of purchasing and installing brand-new UPVC windows, composite doors, or full kitchen replacements. You keep your existing structurally sound units while achieving a brand new modern aesthetic.
How long does the sprayed finish last?
Our industrial-grade 2-pack polyurethane coatings chemically cross-link and bond directly into the UPVC or wood substrate. They are engineered to last 15+ years and come backed by our comprehensive 10-year anti-fade, anti-crack, and anti-peel guarantee.
What colours and finishes can I choose from?
We offer over 1,500 colours across RAL Classic, British Standard (BS), Farrow & Ball, and Dulux colour charts. We can also provide bespoke colour matching to existing decor. Available finishes include Matt, Satin (most popular), and Gloss.
How long does the spraying process take?
Most standard residential projects — including an entire house of external UPVC windows or a full kitchen cabinet respray — are completed in just 1 to 2 days. Single composite doors are typically completed in just a few hours.
Will overspray get on my brickwork, glass, or garden?
Never. Over 60% of our on-site time is dedicated strictly to preparation and protection. We use high-grade electrostatic masking tape, heavy sheeting, and hermetic containment barriers over all glass, brickwork, lawns, and interiors before any spray equipment is switched on.
How quickly can I get a quote?
Simply snap photos of your windows, doors, or kitchen on your phone and send them to us on WhatsApp at 07477 510304. We will review your images and send back a guaranteed, fixed written quote within 15 minutes.
