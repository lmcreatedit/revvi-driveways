Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does UPVC spraying last?
Our specialist coatings chemically bond to the substrate rather than just sitting on top. They come with a 10-year anti-fade and anti-peel guarantee and will maintain their factory look for 15+ years under standard UK weather conditions.
How much can I save compared to replacing windows or doors?
Spraying typically costs 70% to 80% less than replacing windows, doors, or kitchen units. You get the exact same aesthetic transformation and high-end colour finish without paying for new frames or dealing with plaster and structural repair costs.
Will there be any overspray or mess on my property?
None whatsoever. Over 70% of our on-site time is spent precisely masking glass, brickwork, render, floors, and surrounding greenery with heavy-duty plastic sheeting and precision tape before any spray gun is picked up.
What colours and finishes can I choose from?
You can choose any colour from the universal RAL chart (including Anthracite Grey RAL 7016, Jet Black RAL 9005, Agate Grey RAL 7038) or custom matches to Farrow & Ball, Little Greene, and Dulux. Finishes are available in Matte (10%), Satin (30%), or Gloss (70%).
How much disruption will there be in my home?
We keep disruption to an absolute minimum. We do as much prep and work outside as possible, only entering your living spaces when strictly necessary. Everything is sealed with protective masking, and we leave your home tidy and spotless at the end of every day.
Can you spray both the exterior and interior frames?
Yes. We can spray only the external surfaces to modernise your property's curb appeal, or we can spray opening rebated frames, sills, and internal faces for a complete 360-degree transformation.
