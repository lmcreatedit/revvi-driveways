Case Study: Complete Garden & Fencing Overhaul in Wokingham

Location: Wokingham (SL6) • Timeline: 5 Days • Lead Contractor: Mike (Revvi Driveways)

The Brief: An Unusable, Sloping Garden

The homeowners in Wokingham had inherited a tired garden that was unusable for most of the year. The boundary fence was leaning and rotten at the posts, old overgrown shrubs had overtaken the lawn, and rainwater pooled against the back of the house whenever it rained.

They wanted a clean, low-maintenance outdoor living area with a modern entertaining terrace, secure boundary fencing for privacy, and a pristine lawn that wouldn't turn into a mud bath in winter.

The Solution: 5-Day Phased Transformation

Day 1–2: Site Clearance & Foundation Excavation

Stripped back 12 tonnes of old turf, soil, and overgrown roots down to solid ground. Excavated the patio zone to 180mm depth and installed a heavy-duty geotextile membrane with compacted MOT Type 1 aggregate.

Day 3: Heavy-Duty Closeboard Fencing

Erected 1.8m pressure-treated closeboard feather-edge fencing with slotted concrete posts set in deep concrete footings. Fully storm-resistant with integrated gravel boards to keep timber away from ground moisture.

Day 4–5: Vitrified Porcelain Paving & Turfing

Laid 20mm vitrified Italian porcelain on a 5:1 full mortar bed with polymer slurry primer bonding bridges. Finished with external resin grout and laid fresh cultivated lawn turf with clean stone border edges.

The Final Result

The garden was completely transformed from an unusable muddy slope into a clean, modern outdoor living space ready for family dining and summer BBQs. The entire project was delivered on schedule in 5 working days with zero hidden costs.
